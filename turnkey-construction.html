What turnkey means here

Single scope, single accountability.

Turnkey does not mean you lose visibility. It means the coordination burden moves from you to us. You still approve the drawings, the specification and every variation — you simply are not the one chasing an electrician who is waiting on a plasterer.

Where a client prefers to appoint their own architect or supply specific materials, that can be carved out of the scope and documented before the agreement is signed.

Good fit / poor fit

When turnkey is the right choice.

✓

Works well when…

You want one accountable party; you are not on site daily; the scope is reasonably definable up front; you value a predictable cost envelope over maximum flexibility mid-build.

!

Consider alternatives when…

You intend to manage trades yourself; you already have a design team you want to retain contractually; or the scope is expected to change substantially during construction. In those cases a project management or grey structure arrangement may suit better.

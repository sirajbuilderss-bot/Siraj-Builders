Section 06

Client responsibilities

Well-managed projects depend on both sides meeting their responsibilities. Where a client is involved in a project, they are generally expected to:

  • Provide accurate information about the property, requirements and intended use.
  • Make decisions within agreed timeframes so that work is not delayed unnecessarily.
  • Provide access to the property and site as required for the work to proceed.
  • Obtain any approvals, permissions or consents that are the client's responsibility under the project agreement.
  • Meet payment obligations according to the agreed schedule.

Where the client has specific responsibilities, they are set out in the project agreement.

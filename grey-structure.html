Grey Structure

The skeleton a project is built on.

Before finishes, fixtures and fit-outs, a building is a structure. Grey structure covers the load-bearing work — foundation, columns, beams, slabs, blockwork and plaster — executed with coordination and site supervision.

Structural phase only Coordinated on site Scope confirmed per project

Scope to confirm. Grey structure work is offered directly by Siraj Builders, subject to project review. The exact inclusions — excavation, foundations, framing, blockwork, plaster, services chases — are agreed per project after drawings and site conditions are reviewed.

01 · The phase

Structure before finishes. Strength before style.

Grey structure is the phase where the building's shape and strength are established. It is the work that everything else — flooring, tiling, joinery, fittings — is later applied to.

Getting this phase right matters, because structural problems are far more expensive to correct later.

Stage by stage

How the structural phase is organised.

Grey structure isn't one task — it's a sequence of coordinated stages. Each stage should be checked before the next begins.

Stage 01

Setting out

Grid lines, levels and column positions established on site — the reference for everything that follows.

Stage 02

Foundation

Excavation, footings and plinth beams cast to drawings. Structural integrity begins here.

Stage 03

Frame

Columns and beams raised level by level, checked for alignment and level before the next stage.

Stage 04

Slabs & walls

Suspended slabs cast, blockwork laid to agreed layout, services chases planned before plaster.

Stage 05

Plaster & handover to finishes

Internal plaster and external render applied and cured. The structure is ready for the finishing phase.

Why it matters

Structural work is where mistakes cost the most.

Once slabs are cast and walls are plastered, correcting structural issues becomes expensive and disruptive. That's why this phase deserves the most supervision of any part of the project.

What can go wrong
Levels drift

Walls go up slightly off-plumb, doors don't fit, finishes fight the structure.

Concrete mix too weak

Strength assumptions that don't hold up under load or over time.

Services chased late

Walls opened after plaster to run pipes or wires — patched finishes never look the same.

Waterproofing forgotten

Added as an afterthought, or skipped where it matters most.

How we prevent it
Levels checked as work proceeds

Alignment verified stage by stage, not only at handover.

Mix & curing discipline

Concrete placement and curing planned for structural strength.

Services planned before plaster

Chases and conduits coordinated while walls are still open.

Waterproofing at the right stage

Applied before finishes, not added as an afterthought.

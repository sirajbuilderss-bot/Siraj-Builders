What changes with location

Six things that shift from region to region.

These are the practical factors we review for any project, wherever it's built.

Building codes & approvals

Different authorities have different requirements. Approvals and design must align with the local framework.

Material availability

Some materials are local, others need sourcing. Availability and lead times affect both cost and schedule.

Labour & trades

Skill availability and site labour markets vary. Local coordination is planned around what's actually there.

Climate & weather

Heat, rain, humidity and seasonal patterns all influence materials, sequence and site scheduling.

Site access & logistics

Narrow streets, load limits and delivery windows shape how materials and equipment reach the site.

Neighbourhood context

Density, noise restrictions and neighbour expectations change the way a site needs to be managed.

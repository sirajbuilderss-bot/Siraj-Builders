What changes the number

Six things that affect construction cost.

Two projects of the same size can differ significantly in cost. These are the factors that usually explain why.

01

Scope & specification

The biggest driver. A grey-structure build and a fully finished premium home are very different projects.

02

Material selection

Flooring, tiles, fittings, joinery and finishes move the cost more than most clients expect.

03

Site conditions

Soil, access, water table and existing structures can add significant preparation work.

04

Location

Labour availability, transport and local market rates vary between countries, cities and areas.

05

Design complexity

Curves, cantilevers, custom detailing and intricate facades require more time and skill.

06

Timeline & changes

Compressed schedules and scope changes during construction typically increase final cost.

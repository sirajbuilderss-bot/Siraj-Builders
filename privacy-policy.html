Section 02

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly through the website, and information collected automatically when you browse the site.

Information you provide directly may include:

  • Your name and contact details (phone, email) when you submit an enquiry or request a consultation.
  • Project details you choose to share — property location, property size, project type, intended scope, budget range, expected start date and any drawings or documents you send.
  • Any additional information you include in the "project description" or "message" field of our contact and consultation forms.

Information collected automatically may include:

  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location and referring page.
  • Pages visited on this website, time spent and general usage patterns — used only to understand how the site is being used.

Section 03

How we use the information

Information you provide is used only for the purposes for which you shared it — most commonly, to understand your project and respond to your enquiry.

Specifically, we may use the information to:

  • Respond to a project enquiry or consultation request.
  • Understand your property, requirements and intended scope before a first conversation.
  • Prepare or share an initial estimate, drawing review or project-specific response.
  • Keep an internal record of enquiries and correspondence so that future discussions have context.
  • Improve how the website presents information and how our enquiry process works.

We do not sell enquiry information, use it for unrelated marketing, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Section 04

Project enquiry and contact forms

Our contact and consultation forms ask for information that helps us understand your project before a first conversation. The more context you provide, the more useful our response can be.

Once you submit a form, the information is stored securely and used only by the Siraj Builders team or by partners directly involved in responding to your enquiry.

Section 05

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies — small text files stored in your browser — to help the site function properly and to understand general usage patterns.

Cookies on this website may be used to:

  • Remember basic preferences, such as whether you've already seen a particular notice.
  • Measure general traffic — how many people visit, which pages they read, and how they arrive at the site.

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how certain parts of the site behave.

Section 06

Sharing information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Where information is shared, it is shared only with parties who need to know it in order to respond to your enquiry or deliver a project.

This may include:

  • Internal team members or departments directly involved in responding to your enquiry.
  • Confirmed external partners, where their involvement has been agreed with you in advance.
  • Service providers who help run the website or manage enquiries — bound by their own confidentiality obligations.
Confirm any third-party platforms used (hosting, form handling, email) and whether any data is transferred outside your local jurisdiction.
Section 07

Retention

Enquiry information is kept only as long as it serves a clear purpose — most commonly, while a project conversation is active or while a completed project relationship needs to be referenced.

When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised in line with internal practice.

Specify the retention period Siraj Builders applies to enquiry information and any regulatory minimum retention that must be observed.
Section 08

Your rights

You have the right to understand what information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct or remove it where appropriate.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may be able to:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete information you no longer want us to hold.
  • Object to how your information is being used, where the purpose is not essential to serving your enquiry.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details in Section 11.

Confirm the specific rights that apply in Siraj Builders' operating jurisdiction, and any formal process required for handling requests.
Section 09

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, misuse or loss — including access controls, secure connections and internal handling practices.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you have specific concerns about sharing information online, contact us directly using the details below to discuss alternatives.

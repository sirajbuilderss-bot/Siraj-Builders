What residential covers

Six areas of a structured residential build.

Residential construction works across these six areas. Together they take a project from initial requirements to a finished, ready-to-live-in home.

Each area is included where agreed in the project scope. Not every project involves every stage — the scope is confirmed before work begins.
01 / 06

Project planning

Understanding the property, requirements and intended use, then organising scope, drawings and sequence into a plan that can actually be built. Planning is where the hundreds of downstream decisions get made manageable.

02 / 06

Construction coordination

Trades, materials, sequencing and site activities kept aligned throughout the build.

03 / 06

Structural work

Foundation, columns, beams and slabs — the load-bearing phase executed to the agreed drawings.

04 / 06

Finishing

Flooring, surfaces, fittings and details that shape how the finished home actually feels to live in.

05 / 06

Site supervision

Daily site coordination, checks against drawings and early flagging of issues before they compound.

06 / 06

Quality review, communication & handover

Progress updates, decisions recorded as they happen, quality checks before handover and a structured transition to the finished home. The client stays informed throughout — and knows exactly what completion involves.

The workflow

How a residential project moves forward.

A residential project isn't one task — it's a sequence of decisions and stages. Each stage should be agreed before the next begins.

Stage 01

Consultation

Understand your requirements, property and objectives — including how you intend to use the home.

Stage 02

Planning & design

Develop the scope, drawings and specifications — with the decisions that shape the finished home made early.

Stage 03

Construction

Structured execution of the structural work, coordinated on site and checked against drawings as it progresses.

Stage 04

Finishing & detail

Flooring, surfaces, fittings and the details that turn a built structure into a home ready to live in.

Stage 05

Review & handover

Quality review, resolution of outstanding items and a structured handover of the finished property.

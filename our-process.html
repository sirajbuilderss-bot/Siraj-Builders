Our Process

A construction process you can understand.

The purpose of our process is simple: reduce uncertainty. Every stage has a purpose, every decision has an owner, and the client always knows what's happening and what comes next.

Eight structured stages Defined decisions per stage Clear handover
01 · Why a process

Construction doesn't need to feel like guesswork.

Most construction problems aren't technical — they're coordination problems. A decision made out of order. A trade arriving at the wrong time. A material chosen after the drawing that specified it.

A defined process solves most of that. It tells everyone — the client, the site team, the trades and the suppliers — what comes next, who owns it, and what "done" looks like at each stage.

The eight stages

From first conversation to handover.

Every project moves through these eight stages. Each one has a clear purpose, a defined scope, and a decision point before the next stage begins.

Stage 01

Consultation

We start by understanding what you want to build, why you're building it, and what matters most to you — before any technical scope is discussed.

Requirements Intended use
Stage 02

Site assessment

We review the property and identify the practical considerations relevant to the project — access, conditions, existing structures, utilities and constraints.

Site conditions Constraints
Stage 03

Planning & design

The project requirements are translated into an organised scope and, where required, coordinated with the necessary design and planning work.

Scope Drawings Design coordination
Stage 04

Estimation & scope

The project is reviewed in commercial and practical terms so that the client understands what is included, what isn't, and what the work is expected to cost.

Cost Inclusions Agreement
Stage 05

Project preparation

Before execution begins, the required coordination, materials, people and site activities are organised — so the site starts with everything ready, not everything pending.

Materials Sequence Readiness
Stage 06

Construction & supervision

The agreed work moves into execution with appropriate site coordination and supervision — trades, decisions, materials and quality all managed together.

Site coordination Supervision Progress
Stage 07

Quality review

Completed work is reviewed and outstanding issues are identified for resolution — before handover, not after.

Checks Snag list Resolution
Stage 08

Handover

The completed project is reviewed with the client, remaining details are closed out, and the finished property is formally handed over with a documented record.

Final review Documentation Transition
What each stage delivers

Six outcomes that hold the process together.

Across the eight stages, the process produces these outcomes. Together they're what separates a well-managed project from a busy one.

01 / 06

Clarity before commitment

By the end of the estimation stage, the client knows the scope, the cost and the schedule. The commitment is made against a defined picture — not an assumption.

02 / 06

Work in the right order

Trades, decisions and materials move in a sequence — not in collision.

03 / 06

Decisions with a record

Changes, approvals and clarifications captured with a named owner and a documented reason.

04 / 06

Real supervision

Someone is on site, checking work against drawings and catching issues before they compound.

05 / 06

Consistent communication

Progress, decisions and next steps shared throughout — not reconstructed at the end.

06 / 06

Defined handover

A structured completion — final review, resolution of outstanding items and a documented transition. The project actually ends, rather than trailing off.
